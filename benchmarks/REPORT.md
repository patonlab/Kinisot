Addition of the peracid to the carbonyl is rate limiting, so these KIEs probe that step; the alkyl migration comes after it (see baeyer_villiger_migration). All 13C KIEs are relative to the beta carbon (C3/C5), which both papers take as 1.000 (Figure 1a of the 1999 paper, whose text calls the C4 methylene group the internal standard; the 2026 text names C3); the 2H KIEs are relative to the C4 hydrogens. The 1999 rows list the two reactions' KIEs with their standard deviations, as in the paper, and are compared through their mean. The 2026 preprint's Scheme 1c prints 1.008(1) for the alpha carbon, which matches its fit with uncalibrated conversions (1.0087, SI Table S3d); the calibrated value the authors recommend is used here. The 2026 reactions ran at 0 C for 5 h and then at room temperature overnight (SI); the 1999 communication does not state the temperature. 298.15 K is used.
